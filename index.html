From first solve to speed methods

Each method builds on the one before, so you only ever learn a little at a time.

  1. Learn a step

    See where you are going, watch the move in 3D, then try every case on your own cube.

  2. Solve your own cube

    Scan or paint your cube and follow the moves, written exactly as the lessons teach them.

  3. Get faster

    Move on to CFOP or Roux, drill algorithms in the trainer and time your solves.

Installing and updating

Cubewise is not on Google Play, so your phone asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Download the app file

    Tap Download on your phone. The file is called Cubewise.apk and is about 2.7 MB. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser to install apps. Allow it, go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect steps in, choose More details, then Install anyway.

  3. Updating later

    In Settings, tap Check for updates. Cubewise downloads the new version and Android installs it over the old one, keeping everything.

Questions

Is Cubewise really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription, no trial and no adverts, and nothing is locked. Every method, lesson and feature is in the free app.

Is it on Google Play?

No. Cubewise is a personal project and is downloaded from this site as an Android app file. It needs Android 8 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. Lessons, solving, the timer and the trainer all work with no connection. The app goes online only when you tap Check for updates.

Is the camera picture sent anywhere?

No. While you scan, each frame is read on the phone for the colours of the squares and then thrown away. Nothing is saved or uploaded.

Which cubes does it solve?

Standard 2x2, 3x3, 4x4 and 5x5 cubes with the usual colours: white opposite yellow, green opposite blue, red opposite orange. You can pick shades that match your own cube.

Which method should I start with?

The 3x3 Beginner method: eight short steps, each with its own story and only a handful of moves. Each method after it builds on the one before.

What if I change phones?

In Settings, choose Make a backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as Google Drive. Install Cubewise there and choose Restore.

How do I get updates?

In Settings, tap Check for updates. If there is a new version, Cubewise downloads it and Android asks you to install it over the old one. Everything is kept.

Who made it?
