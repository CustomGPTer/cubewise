Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy covers the Cubewise app for Android (package uk.jj.cubewise) and this website. Cubewise is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Your settings, lessons done, cubes, solve times and trainer progress are kept on your phone. They are never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • Cubewise goes online only when you tap Check for updates.
  • The camera picture is read on your phone while you scan and is not kept or uploaded.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a backup file or a cheat sheet you share.

What stays on your phone

Cubewise keeps its files in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. They are left out of phone cloud backups.

  • Your settings and which lessons you have marked done.
  • The last cube of each size you scanned, painted or mixed up, as a string of colour letters.
  • Your solve times, with their scrambles and dates.
  • Your algorithm trainer progress.

The camera

The camera is used only while you scan a cube. Each frame is read on the phone for the average colour of each square of the grid, then thrown away. No photo or video is ever saved or sent.

When Cubewise uses the network

Cubewise holds Android's internet permission for one thing only.

  • Check for updates. Only when you tap it, Cubewise asks GitHub, where it is published, for the latest version. If you choose to download it, the app file comes from GitHub. GitHub sees an ordinary web request, including your IP address, as any website would. Nothing about you or your cubes is sent. GitHub's own privacy statement covers what it keeps.

Opening More apps or a website opens your own browser.

Permissions

  • Camera: scanning a cube, only while you scan.
  • Internet: the update check.
  • Install apps: handing a downloaded update to Android's installer. You confirm every install.
  • Vibration: a gentle buzz on each turn, if you want it.

Your choices

Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is nothing for the developer to show you or delete. You can delete everything by uninstalling Cubewise, or by clearing its storage in your phone's settings.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. GitHub may keep server logs, as described in its own privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
