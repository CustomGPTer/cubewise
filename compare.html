What else Cubewise brings

Eleven methods taught step by step with 3D animations, from the 3x3 beginner method to Yau5, solves that follow the method you are learning, parity explained on 4x4 and 5x5, a timer, an algorithm trainer and printable cheat sheets, all free.

Where the others do more

Both are on Google Play and update themselves, and both solve more kinds of puzzle: Cube Cipher's listing names the Pyraminx and Skewb as well as cubes. Cubewise covers 2x2 to 5x5 cubes.
