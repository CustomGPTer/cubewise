First release of Cubewise, a free Android app that teaches you to solve 2x2 to 5x5 cubes, with no adverts and nothing to unlock.

- Eleven methods, easiest first: 2x2 Beginner, Ortega and CLL; 3x3 Beginner, 4-Look CFOP, Full CFOP and Roux; 4x4 Reduction and Yau; 5x5 Reduction and Yau5.
- Lessons for every step with 3D animations, arrows, hand guides and hold-it-like-this poses; moves in words, letters or both.
- Galleries of all 57 OLL, 21 PLL, 41 F2L and 42 2x2 CLL cases.
- Solve my cube for 2x2 to 5x5: camera scan read on the phone, painting or the virtual cube; checks for impossible cubes; solves in the method you are learning, or the shortest route; 4x4 and 5x5 parity explained.
- Virtual cube for every size, a timer with scrambles, inspection and averages, and an algorithm trainer.
- Cheat sheets in the app, as a printable PDF and as a wallet card; sticker shades to match your cube; colour-blind symbols; dark and light themes.
- Backup and restore in one file, an update check and a list of more apps.

Android 8 or later. SHA-256 of Cubewise.apk: see the release notes page at https://customgpter.github.io/cubewise/releases.html
