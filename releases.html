Release notes

What changed in each version of Cubewise. The newest is at the top.

Download the latest version

Version 1.0.0. Android app file, 2.7 MB. Android 8 or later. Free.

Version 1.0.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Eleven methods, easiest first: 2x2 Beginner, Ortega and CLL; 3x3 Beginner, 4-Look CFOP, Full CFOP and Roux; 4x4 Reduction and Yau; 5x5 Reduction and Yau5.
  • Lessons for every step with 3D animations, arrows, hand guides and hold-it-like-this poses; moves in words, letters or both.
  • Galleries of all 57 OLL, 21 PLL, 41 F2L and 42 2x2 CLL cases.
  • Solve my cube for 2x2 to 5x5: camera scan read on the phone, painting, or the virtual cube; checks for impossible cubes; solves in the chosen method or the shortest route; 4x4 OLL and PLL parity and 5x5 edge parity explained.
  • Virtual cube for every size, a timer with scrambles, inspection and averages, and an algorithm trainer.
  • Cheat sheets in the app, as a printable PDF and as a wallet card; sticker shades to match your cube; colour-blind symbols; dark and light themes.
  • Backup and restore in one file, an update check and a list of more apps.

Checking your download

Every version is signed with the same key, so an update installs over the last one and keeps your progress. Android refuses an update signed by anyone else.

If you want to check the file itself, its SHA-256 checksum is:

55401e7b559787a86fb3849a829de9c8158cb01d1241a7f1f2d61f2a5c26b5cd

Older versions, if any, are on the releases page on GitHub.
